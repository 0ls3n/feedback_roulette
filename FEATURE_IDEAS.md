# Feature Ideas - Making Feedback Roulette Addicting

1. **Streaks** - Track consecutive days of giving feedback. Show a streak counter with a "don't break it" nudge. Losable progress is a powerful retention driver.

2. **Leaderboard tiers, not just ranks** - The Leaderboard page exists. Add tier badges (Bronze/Silver/Gold) based on feedback given. Tiers decay without activity - forces return visits.

3. **Random bonus credits** - When reviewing, occasionally award a "bonus spin" with 2x-5x credits. Variable-ratio rewards are the most addicting reinforcement schedule (this is literally the "roulette" concept - lean into it).

4. **Unlockable profile cosmetics** - Spend credits not just on receiving feedback, but on avatar borders, profile themes, badges. Gives high-feedback-givers something to spend surplus credits on.

5. **Reciprocity notifications** - When someone gives you feedback, notify with "They spent 5 minutes on your work - consider returning the favor." Social obligation drives return visits.

6. **Review queue urgency** - Show "3 submissions waiting for feedback" with a decay timer. New uploads that don't receive feedback within X hours get highlighted. FOMO for givers.

7. **Feedback quality scores** - Let receivers rate feedback as helpful/not. Top reviewers get a "Trusted Reviewer" badge and their own submissions get priority in the roulette.

8. **Daily challenge** - "Review 3 submissions today for a 50-credit bonus." Simple daily goals with clear rewards.
